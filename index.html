CropRoute

/ˈkrɒp·ruːt/ · noun
  1. 1The path a crop travels from root to market.
  2. 2The network of people who move it — the farmer who grows it, the retailer who supplies it, the trader who buys it, the processor who transforms it.
  3. 3The company that connects the stops on that path.

The Route

Every crop in India travels a route. At every stop, information, credit and trust fail to travel to the next.

  1. 01FieldAdvice, input availability and fair prices rarely travel back to the farm gate.
  2. 02AggregationProduce changes hands on memory and word of mouth, with no record both sides can see.
  3. 03MandiPrices are discovered in the yard, but seldom travel beyond it in time to matter.
  4. 04TradeCredit runs on personal relationships, so working capital stops where trust runs out.
  5. 05ProcessingQuality and volume upstream stay invisible until the truck arrives at the gate.
  6. 06ExportTraceability demanded downstream cannot be assembled from paper records upstream.

About

CropRoute is being built by an operator who has worked every stop of the route — field sales, farmer platforms, farm mechanisation, and food value chains and export.

Independent research on the farmer–retailer interface — three papers — underpins the first product. Field research for CropRoute Dukaan runs through kharif 2026.
